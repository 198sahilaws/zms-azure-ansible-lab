Try breaking it

On the host running any one service: sudo systemctl stop zms-catalog

Reload this page. The catalog card turns red, the other two keep their numbers, and Products still lists rows with a dash in the stock column instead of returning a 500. Start it again and the estate heals with no other action — or wait for the next converge, which starts it for you.
