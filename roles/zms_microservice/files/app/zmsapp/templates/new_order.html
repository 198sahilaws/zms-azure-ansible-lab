Worth trying

Pick a product the products page shows as out of stock and order it. inventory-svc returns 409, orders-svc refuses, and no order row is written — the failure is caught before anything is committed anywhere.

Then stop inventory-svc entirely and try again: orders-svc gets a timeout instead of a 409 and returns 503. Same refusal, different reason, and the message above tells you which.
